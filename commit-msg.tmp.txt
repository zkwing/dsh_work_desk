fix(ui): follow the host theme, frost the cards, densify the grid

- Every surface, label, border, accent, and state color now comes from the DSH
  alias tokens (--dsw-*), declared once on :root so the portaled picker dialog
  inherits them too. The panel no longer forces `color-scheme: dark`, which is
  the layout theme presenter's job on <html>, so the panel is correct in the
  light palette, the dark palette, and under a registered third-party theme.
- Cards — and the plus card and the empty state — are frosted glass: backdrop
  blur with saturation, a lit top edge, an inner highlight, a pooled shadow, and
  a specular sheen that drifts across on hover. The tint is mixed from the label
  color, so one recipe gives dark-tinted glass in the light palette and
  light-tinted glass in the dark one.
- The background grid is denser (16px cells over an 80px block; it was 32px) and
  switches color by scheme: white on the dark palette, gold rgb(176,128,24) on
  the light one.
- The scheme arrives as a live `hooks.scheme` source (bound to `useScheme`),
  seeded from ctx.theme.getTheme().active.colorScheme and flipped by the
  `theme/change` event, so the grid follows a live theme switch.

Also fixes scripts/check-file-routes.mjs: it now uses the workspace registry's
own session->workspace mapping instead of falling back to an arbitrary row, so
it cannot pick a subagent session whose boundary the Host resolves differently
and then report a boundary refusal that is not a defect.

verify-artifact.mjs: 140 checks (was 137).
